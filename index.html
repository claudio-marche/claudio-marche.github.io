Professional profile

Claudio Marche is a fixed-term researcher (RTDa) in Telecommunications (Italian Academic Sector IINF-03/A) at the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering (DIEE) of the University of Cagliari, where he is a member of the Net4U (Networks for Humans Laboratory) research group and of the Italian National Inter-University Consortium for Telecommunications (CNIT). He received the M.Sc. degree in Telecommunications Engineering with highest honours in 2018 and the Ph.D. degree in Electronic and Computer Engineering in 2023, both from the University of Cagliari. In 2021–2022, he was a visiting Ph.D. student at the Inria Centre at Lille (FUN Team), France.

His research activity lies in the Internet of Things (IoT) field and develops along four main research lines: trustworthiness management in communications, navigability of Social Internet of Things (SIoT) networks, Digital Twins and their management in telecommunication networks, and service orchestration across the edge–cloud continuum. He is co-author of numerous publications in international journals including IEEE Transactions on Network and Service Management, IEEE Transactions on Services Computing, IEEE Internet of Things Journal, and Computer Networks, as well as papers in major IEEE ComSoc conferences including ICC, Globecom, and WF-IoT. He actively contributes to the scientific community through editorial and organisational activities: he is an Associate Editor of the IEEE Internet of Things Journal and a member of the Topical Advisory Panel of MDPI Sensors, and has served as Guest Editor of Special Issues devoted to trustworthiness in IoT, Digital Twins, and 5G/6G communications. He has held organisational roles in international conferences, including General Co-Chair of MedComNet 2025, and serves on the Technical Program Committees of ICC and Globecom. He is an IEEE member and a member of IEEE ComSoc Technical Committees (AHSN, WTC, TCSN), serves as secretary of two IEEE technical groups, and has been invited as a keynote and tutorial speaker at several international conferences on trustworthiness in IoT and Digital Twins.
